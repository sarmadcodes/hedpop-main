Common questions

How do I make a booking?

Open a salon, choose a service, then pick a date and time and tap Confirm Booking. You will see a reference number and the booking appears in My Bookings.

How do I pay?

You pay the salon directly when you attend. The app does not take payments, and the price shown is the amount to pay at the salon.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to My Bookings and cancel the booking there. Cancellation in the app is available within 1 hour of confirming. After that, please contact the salon or email us.

I forgot my password

Email hedpop81@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will help you regain access. If you are signed in, you can change your password in Profile, then Privacy & Security.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app go to Profile, then Privacy & Security, then Delete Account, and enter your password. Your profile, bookings, favourites, loyalty points and notifications are permanently deleted. If you cannot sign in, email us from your account address and we will delete it for you.

I am not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for HedPop in your phone Settings, then sign out and back in so your device is registered again.
