Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the HedPop app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and we do not sell your data.

Who we are

HedPop LTD ("we", "us") runs the HedPop app, which lets you find barbers and salons and book appointments. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: hedpop81@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Name, email address, phone numberTo create your account, sign you in and let salons identify your booking.
Address (if you add one)To store your details on your profile.
Gender / style preferenceTo choose the app's look and show relevant services.
PasswordStored only in hashed form so we can verify you. We cannot read it.
Bookings, favourites, loyalty points, notificationsTo run the booking service and show your history.
Device push tokenTo send booking notifications to your phone (see Service providers).
Technical logs (IP address, request time)To keep the service secure and fix faults.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or payment card details. The app does not take card payments: you pay the salon directly.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app: accounts, bookings, cancellations and notifications.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To reply when you contact support.

Our legal bases under UK GDPR are performing our contract with you (running your account and bookings) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

No tracking. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not share your data for marketing.

Who sees your data

  • The salon you book with sees your name, contact details and booking so they can serve you.
  • Service providers process data for us: Google Firebase delivers push notifications to your device, and our hosting provider runs our servers.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, bookings, favourites, loyalty points and notifications are permanently deleted. Routine server logs are kept for a short period and then removed.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Privacy & Security, then Delete Account, and enter your password. If you cannot sign in, email hedpop81@gmail.com from your account address.

Children

HedPop is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to our servers use HTTPS, and passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
